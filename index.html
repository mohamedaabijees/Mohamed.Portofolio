About Me

Mindset & Engineering Philosophy

"A system-oriented developer with a business-driven mindset — I optimize for outcomes, not just code."

Most developers write code. I architect outcomes. By applying Systems Thinking to every engagement and a Product-Oriented Mindset to every decision, I bridge the gap between technical complexity and the business value that stakeholders actually need.

Based in Hargeisa, Somaliland, I build technology solutions deeply contextualized for the East African market — integrating local payment systems like EVC Plus and Zaad/Telesom, local languages, and real infrastructure constraints that global templates ignore.
